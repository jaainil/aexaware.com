# auth.md

Aexaware Infotech does not operate an OAuth authorization server or require authentication for any public content.

## Access

All pages and resources on **https://aexaware.com** are publicly accessible without authentication.

AI agents may access all public content freely. No registration or credentials are required to read, index, or cite content from this website.
