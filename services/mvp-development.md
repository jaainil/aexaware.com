## What is MVP Development?
“
A Proof of Concept (PoC) determines feasibility—whether your software idea can be developed within budget and fills a market gap. The next step is creating a Minimum Viable Product (MVP): a test product with minimum features to validate your idea and estimate development parameters. Typically, an MVP is created within a very short duration—generally not more than a couple of months—to derive insights about your final marketable product and how you'll develop it.
