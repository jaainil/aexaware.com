Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a custom web application?
Project timelines vary based on complexity. A simple website takes 4-6 weeks, while complex web applications can take 3-6 months. We provide detailed timelines after the discovery phase.
Which platform should we choose for our store or content site?
Shopify is the fastest path to selling online, WooCommerce suits WordPress-first teams, and headless CMS plus a custom frontend wins on performance and flexibility. We recommend based on your budget, content workflow, and growth plans.
What is the cost of web development services?
Costs depend on project scope, features, and complexity. We offer flexible pricing models: fixed-price for defined projects, time-and-material for evolving requirements, and dedicated team models for long-term partnerships.
Can you migrate our existing website or CMS without losing SEO?
Yes. We handle data migration, URL redirects, and content preservation so rankings survive the move. We've migrated sites from WordPress, PHP, and legacy platforms to Next.js, React, and Astro.
Do you provide post-launch support and maintenance?
Yes, we offer 90-day post-launch warranty and ongoing maintenance packages. Our support includes security updates, performance monitoring, bug fixes, and feature enhancements.
