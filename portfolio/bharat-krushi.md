### Project Goals

 - Enable **direct-to-customer product visibility** for Bharat Krushi’s bio-fertilizer range
 - Provide a **simple and manageable e-commerce system** for the internal team
 - Ensure **fast performance**, SEO readiness, and long-term scalability
 - Build a platform suitable for **future online sales growth** without technical complexity

---

### Implementation

**Platform Selection**

 - Chose **WordPress 6.8.2** with **WooCommerce 10.1.3** for its flexibility, cost efficiency, and ease of management
 - Used **Astra theme** with **Elementor** for fast-loading, customizable layouts

**UI & Content Structure**

 - Designed clean product pages with clear descriptions and usage information
 - Structured the website for corporate credibility, product discovery, and trust-building
 - Implemented responsive layouts to ensure smooth browsing across devices

**E-Commerce Setup**

 - Configured WooCommerce for product catalog management
 - Organized product categories and individual product layouts
 - Prepared checkout and cart flow for future enhancements

**Performance & Infrastructure**

 - Deployed on **LiteSpeed server** with **LiteSpeed Cache** for optimized delivery
 - Enabled **HTTP/3**, **Priority Hints**, and CDN-level optimizations via Hostinger
 - Reduced load times and improved Core Web Vitals

**SEO & Discoverability**

 - Implemented **Yoast SEO** for metadata, sitemap generation, and Open Graph support
 - Optimized URL structures and content hierarchy
 - Enabled RSS feeds for content distribution

---

### Outcome

 - Delivered a **stable, fast, and scalable e-commerce-ready website**
 - Improved product visibility and brand credibility online
 - Enabled the internal team to manage products and content without developer dependency
 - Established a **strong digital foundation** for future D2C sales growth
