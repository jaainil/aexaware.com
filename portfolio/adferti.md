## Case Study: ADFerti – Corporate Website & E-Commerce Platform

**Client:** ADFerti **Project Status:** Successfully handed over last month **Industry:** Agriculture / Fertilizers

---

### Project Objective

ADFerti required a modern, reliable digital platform to establish its online presence, showcase fertilizer products clearly, and support future online sales. The goal was to create a **clean, fast, and SEO-ready website** that could be easily managed by a non-technical team.

---

## What Aexaware Infotech Did

### 1. Website Architecture & CMS Setup

 - Set up **WordPress 6.9** as the core CMS to ensure ease of content management
 - Structured pages for company overview, product listings, and blog content
 - Configured a scalable architecture suitable for future expansion

---

### 2. Custom UI Implementation

 - Built the website using the **Blocksy theme**, customized to match ADFerti’s branding
 - Implemented layouts using **Elementor** for flexible page management
 - Ensured consistent typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy across pages

---

### 3. E-Commerce Configuration

 - Integrated **WooCommerce 10.4.2** for product management
 - Configured product categories, individual product layouts, and pricing structure
 - Prepared the platform for future payment gateway and checkout enhancements

---

### 4. Performance & Hosting Optimization

 - Deployed the website on **Hostinger** with **Hostinger CDN**
 - Enabled **Priority Hints** and HTTP/3 support for faster page loading
 - Optimized assets and scripts to improve Core Web Vitals

---

### 5. SEO & Discoverability

 - Implemented **RankMath SEO** for on-page SEO configuration
 - Set up metadata, Open Graph tags, and XML sitemaps
 - Optimized URL structure and content hierarchy for search engines

---

### 6. Technical Stack & Stability

 - Backend powered by **PHP 8.2** and **MySQL**
 - Frontend enhancements using **jQuery and core-js** for compatibility
 - Ensured cross-browser and device responsiveness

---

### 7. Handover & Client Enablement

 - Final testing across devices and browsers
 - Clean handover with admin access and usage guidance
 - Delivered a maintainable platform requiring minimal ongoing technical support

---

## Result

 - A **stable, fast, and professional corporate website**
 - Structured product presentation ready for scale
 - SEO-ready foundation for organic growth
 - Easy day-to-day content and product management for the client
