## The Challenge

J K Fertilizers required a robust digital platform to showcase their extensive range of fertilizer products and connect with farmers and dealers across the region. Their existing presence was minimal, and they needed a solution that was both informative and easy to navigate for a diverse audience.

## Our Solution

We developed a custom web application using React and Tailwind CSS, focusing on performance and accessibility. Key features include:

- **Interactive Product Catalog**: Allows users to filter products by crop type and application.
- **Dealer Locator**: Integrated Google Maps to help farmers find the nearest authorized dealers.
- **Multilingual Support**: To cater to the local farming community.

## Results

The new website has significantly improved brand visibility and dealer engagement.

- **40% increase** in online inquiries.
- **Fast load times** even on 3G networks.
