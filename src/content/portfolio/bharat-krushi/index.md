## Project Goals

Enable direct-to-consumer sales for Bharat Krushi's bio-fertilizer range. The platform needed to be robust, secure, and easy for the internal team to manage.

## Implementation

We chose Shopify for its reliable e-commerce infrastructure and built a custom theme to match the sustainability-focused brand identity.

- **Custom Liquid Development**: Tailored product pages to show detailed usage instructions.
- **Inventory Sync**: Automated synchronization with their warehouse management system.
- **Mobile First**: Optimized checkout flow for mobile users.

## Outcome

Sales volume increased by **200%** within the first quarter of launch.
