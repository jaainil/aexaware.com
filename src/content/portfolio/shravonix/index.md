## Case Study: Shravonix Website & CMS Implementation

### Client Overview

**Shravonix** is a technology-focused brand that required a modern, high-performance digital presence to publish content, communicate expertise, and scale seamlessly as the platform grows.

Aexaware Infotech partnered with Shravonix to design and develop a custom website experience backed by a robust content management system and an optimized delivery stack.

***

### Business Objectives

* Establish a **clean, professional, and scalable website** aligned with Shravonix’s brand identity
* Enable **easy content publishing** through a powerful CMS
* Ensure **high performance, global delivery, and reliability**
* Support blogs, comments, analytics, and SEO-friendly distribution
* Build a **custom theme** instead of relying on off-the-shelf templates

***

### Solution Delivered

#### 1. Custom Website & Theme Development

Aexaware designed and developed a **fully custom Ghost theme**, tailored specifically for Shravonix’s branding, typography, and content structure.
The theme was built with performance and maintainability in mind, ensuring consistency across pages while allowing flexibility for future enhancements.

#### 2. CMS & Content Architecture

We implemented **Ghost CMS (v6.8)** to power the website and blog infrastructure. Ghost was selected for its speed, simplicity, and excellent publishing workflow, enabling the Shravonix team to manage blogs and content independently without technical overhead.

#### 3. Modern Web Stack

The platform was engineered using a modern JavaScript-based architecture to ensure scalability and performance:

* **Frontend:** React for dynamic and responsive UI components
* **Backend:** Node.js with Express for server-side logic
* **Web Framework:** Express-based delivery for flexibility and control

#### 4. Performance, CDN & Delivery Optimization

To ensure fast global access and resilience, the website was integrated with multiple CDN and optimization layers:

* Cloudflare (CDN, HTTP/3 support, performance optimization)
* jsDelivr, cdnjs, and Bunny for asset delivery
* Optimized font delivery using Bunny Fonts and Google Font API

#### 5. Analytics & Real User Monitoring

We integrated **Cloudflare Browser Insights** to capture real user performance metrics, allowing visibility into load times, user experience, and browser-level performance.

#### 6. Community & Engagement Features

* **Giscus** was implemented as the comment system to enable community discussion on blog posts
* **RSS & Open Graph** support was configured to improve content syndication and social sharing
