## Overview

SV Engineering needed a modern corporate identity to reflect their cutting-edge engineering solutions. We built a high-performance website using Next.js and TypeScript, ensuring type safety and SEO optimization.

## Key Features

- **Project Gallery**: A dynamic portfolio section to showcase their past engineering marvels.
- **Smooth Animations**: Utilized Framer Motion for subtle entry animations that enhance the user experience without distracting from the content.
- **Service Detail Pages**: In-depth pages for each engineering discipline.

## Impact

The professional look and updated portfolio helped SV Engineering secure contracts with tier-1 construction firms.
